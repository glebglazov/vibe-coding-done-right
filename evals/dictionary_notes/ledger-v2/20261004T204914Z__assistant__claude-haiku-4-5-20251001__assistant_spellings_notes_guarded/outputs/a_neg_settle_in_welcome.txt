Welcome to the team! We're thrilled to have you here.

Your first week is all about getting oriented, so don't worry about being productive right away. Here's what to expect:

**Your First Days**
- Meet your team and key contacts across the company
- Set up your workspace, accounts, and tools
- Review our onboarding materials and company handbook

- Your buddy or mentor is here to answer questions—use them freely
- IT support can help with any technical setup issues
- HR is available for paperwork and benefits questions

**A Few Tips**
- It's completely normal to feel overwhelmed—that passes quickly
- Take notes, ask questions, and don't hesitate to reach out
- We're all here to help you succeed
