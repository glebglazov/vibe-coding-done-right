Welcome to the team! We're thrilled to have you here.

Your first week is all about getting oriented and settling in. Here's what to expect:

**Day One**
- Meet your manager and team
- Get your workspace and equipment set up
- Complete onboarding essentials
- Grab lunch with a colleague if you can

**Your First Week**
- Attend key orientation sessions
- Review our handbook and policies
- Connect with people across departments
- Don't hesitate to ask questions—everyone's here to help

**Quick Tips**
- Bookmark important resources and Slack channels
- Schedule 1:1s with your team members early
- Take notes on processes and names (no judgment)
- Be patient with yourself—there's a lot to learn

Remember: everyone on this team was new once. We're genuinely happy you're here, and we're committed to making your transition smooth. If you need anything at all—whether it's technical help, finding the bathroom, or clarifying how we do things—just ask.

Looking forward to working with you!
