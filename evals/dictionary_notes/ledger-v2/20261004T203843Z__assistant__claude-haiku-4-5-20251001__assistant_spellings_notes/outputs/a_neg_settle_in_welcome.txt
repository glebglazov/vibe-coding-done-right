# Welcome to the Team!

We're thrilled to have you join us. Your first week is an exciting time, and we want to make sure you feel supported every step of the way.

**Here's what to expect:**
- Your manager will guide you through onboarding and connect you with key people on the team
- Don't hesitate to ask questions — there's no such thing as a silly one
- Take time to explore our workspace, grab coffee with colleagues, and get a feel for how we work
- We'll share access to tools, documentation, and resources you'll need

**A few tips for settling in:**
- Keep a small notebook handy to jot down names, processes, and useful information
- Attend team meetings to understand our priorities and culture
- Introduce yourself in our main chat channel — let us know a bit about your background
- Schedule 1-on-1s with teammates early; people genuinely enjoy meeting newcomers

**Remember:** Everyone here started exactly where you are. We're invested in your success and want you to feel at home quickly. If you need anything at all — whether it's technical support, a recommendation for lunch, or just a friendly face — reach out.

We're excited to see what you'll contribute. Welcome aboard!
