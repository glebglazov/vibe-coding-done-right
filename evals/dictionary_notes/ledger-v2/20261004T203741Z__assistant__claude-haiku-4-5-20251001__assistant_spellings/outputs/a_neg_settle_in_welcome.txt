Welcome to the team! We're excited to have you on board.

Your first week is all about getting oriented and meeting the people you'll be working with. Don't worry about being productive yet—focus on understanding how things work here. Here are a few things that might help:

**Getting Started**
- Your onboarding contact will guide you through setup and access to tools you'll need
- Ask questions freely—everyone remembers being new
- Check out our internal wiki or documentation for company policies and processes

**People & Culture**
- Make time to grab coffee or lunch with your team members
- Attend any team meetings or all-hands sessions on the calendar
- Join relevant Slack channels to stay connected

**Your First Tasks**
- Set up your workspace and equipment
- Complete any required admin or compliance training
- Schedule 1-on-1 time with your manager to align on priorities and expectations

**Quick Tips**
- Take notes on new information—it's a lot to absorb
- Don't hesitate to ask for help or clarification
- The team genuinely wants you to succeed

We're glad you're here. Welcome aboard!
