**Welcome to the Team!**

We're thrilled to have you join us. Your first week is about getting comfortable, meeting your team, and learning how things work here—so don't worry about being perfect right away.

**Here's what to expect:**
- Your manager will help you get set up and walk you through the essentials
- Take time to explore our spaces, systems, and resources
- Introduce yourself to your teammates—we're a friendly group
- Ask questions freely; that's how you learn fastest

**A few tips:**
- Check your email for important onboarding documents and calendar invites
- Grab coffee or lunch with colleagues when you can
- Keep a list of questions for your one-on-ones
- Be patient with yourself as you settle in

We're here to support you. If you need anything, don't hesitate to reach out to your manager or the people around you.

Here's to a great start. Welcome aboard!
