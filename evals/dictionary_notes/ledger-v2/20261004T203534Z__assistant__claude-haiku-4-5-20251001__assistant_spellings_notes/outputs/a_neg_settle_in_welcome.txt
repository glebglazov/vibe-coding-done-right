Welcome to the team! We're thrilled to have you here.

Your first week is all about getting comfortable and finding your footing. Here's what you can expect:

**Your First Days**
You'll meet your team, get your workspace set up, and receive an overview of how we work. Don't hesitate to ask questions—everyone remembers their first week, and we're here to help.

**What to Focus On**
- Learn the names and roles of your teammates
- Familiarize yourself with our tools and systems
- Review key documentation and processes
- Connect with your direct manager about priorities

**Quick Tips**
- Grab coffee or lunch with teammates when you can—it's the best way to build relationships
- Keep a running list of questions; save them for a dedicated sync with your manager
- Be patient with yourself; everything will start clicking faster than you think

**We're Here For You**
If you need anything—whether it's technical help, directions to the kitchen, or just a friendly face—reach out. We want you to succeed, and supporting new team members is something we take seriously.

Looking forward to working with you!
