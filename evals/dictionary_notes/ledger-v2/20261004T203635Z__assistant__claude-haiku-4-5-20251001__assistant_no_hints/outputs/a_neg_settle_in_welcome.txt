# Welcome to the Team!

We're thrilled to have you join us. Your first week is all about getting comfortable, so don't hesitate to ask questions—no question is too small.

**Here's what to expect:**
- Your team will help you get set up with systems and access
- We'll introduce you to key colleagues and walk you through how we work
- You'll get a sense of our culture and rhythm

**A few tips:**
- Bring a notepad to meetings—you'll pick up a lot
- Grab coffee or lunch with your teammates when you can
- Focus on listening and learning; you're not expected to be productive immediately

**We're here for you.** If you need anything—technical help, clarification on processes, or just a friendly face—reach out to your manager or any team member. We're all invested in making your onboarding smooth.

Looking forward to working with you.
