Welcome to the team! We're excited to have you on board.

During your first week, here's what you can expect:

**Getting Started**
You'll meet your team, receive your equipment, and get access to the systems you'll need. Don't hesitate to ask questions — everyone here remembers their first week.

**What Helps**
- Keep a list of people you meet and what they do
- Explore our documentation and internal resources
- Attend any scheduled onboarding sessions
- Set up 1-on-1 meetings with your manager and key teammates

**Your First Days**
Focus on listening and learning rather than producing. Understand how things work, what priorities matter, and who does what. Small wins come later.

**We're Here for You**
Reach out to your manager, your onboarding buddy, or anyone on the team with questions. There's no such thing as a silly question in your first week.

We believe you'll do great things here. Welcome aboard!
