Welcome to the team! We're excited to have you on board.

Your first week is all about getting oriented and meeting the people you'll be working with. Here's what to expect:

**Day One:** You'll complete onboarding paperwork, get your equipment set up, and meet your manager and immediate team. Don't worry about jumping into projects yet—focus on learning how things work here.

**First Week:** You'll have time to explore our systems, attend introductory meetings, and start understanding the workflows. Ask questions freely—that's what your first week is for. Everyone here remembers being new.

**Practical Tips:**
- Keep a list of questions as they come up—we'll schedule time to answer them
- Introduce yourself to people in adjacent teams; those connections matter
- Review any documentation we've sent you, but ask if anything's unclear
- Lunch with the team is a great way to get to know people informally

You'll likely feel a bit overwhelmed at times—that's completely normal. Your ramp-up period is built in, and we're here to support you through it. If you need anything or hit a snag, reach out to your manager or anyone on the team.

We're looking forward to working with you. Welcome aboard!
