My grandmother used to make this amazing soup, and I've been trying to recreate it for years. The base is simple—it's just onions, carrots, celery, the usual. But the secret was miso paste—I think—which was weird because she wasn't into Asian cooking at all. Someone must have told her about it at some point. She'd let it simmer for hours—not 30 minutes like the recipes online say, but three or four. The texture would get thick, almost stew-like. I tried it last weekend and it was close, but not quite right. I think I need to use a different kind of potato. She used waxy ones, not the russets I had.
