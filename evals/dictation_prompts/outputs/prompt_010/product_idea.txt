Okay, here's an idea I had. What if we built a browser extension that tracks how much time you spend on different sites, but instead of just showing you a boring chart, it actually calculates how much money that time cost based on your hourly rate? If you spend 45 minutes on Twitter, it would say "hey, that just cost you $37" or whatever. I know there's time trackers out there already, but none of them do the money angle in an in-your-face way. The psychological impact of seeing dollars instead of minutes could be really powerful, I think, although some people might find it depressing. But that's the point, right?
