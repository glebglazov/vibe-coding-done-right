We spend so much time optimizing for productivity, but what are we even being productive for? I read an article the other day that said people on their deathbed never say "I wish I'd been more productive" — they say "I wish I'd spent more time with people I love." There's something to that. But you can't just not work — that's not realistic. So the question becomes: how do you find the balance? I don't think anyone's really figured that out yet. Maybe the answer is different for everyone. It's just something I've been thinking about lately, especially with everything going on.
