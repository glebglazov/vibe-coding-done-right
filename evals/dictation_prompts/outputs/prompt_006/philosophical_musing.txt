You know what's weird—we spend so much time optimizing for productivity, but what are we even being productive for? I read this article the other day that said people on their deathbed never say, "I wish I'd been more productive." They say, "I wish I'd spent more time with people I love." And I think there's something to that, but also you can't just not work—that's not realistic. So the question becomes how do you find the balance? And I don't think anyone's really figured that out yet. Maybe the answer is different for everyone. I don't know, it's just something I've been thinking about lately, especially with everything going on.
