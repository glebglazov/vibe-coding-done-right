So my grandmother used to make this amazing soup, and I've been trying to recreate it for years. The base is pretty simple: it's just onions, carrots, celery, the usual. But the secret was she would add miso paste, which is weird because she wasn't into Asian cooking at all, but someone must have told her about it at some point. And she would let it simmer for hours. Not 30 minutes like the recipes online say, but actually three or four hours. And the texture would get so thick, almost like a stew. I tried it last weekend and it was close but not quite right. I think maybe I need to use a different kind of potato because she used those waxy ones, not the russet ones I had.
