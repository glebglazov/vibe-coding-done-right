What's weird is we spend so much time optimizing for productivity, but what are we even being productive for? I read an article the other day about people on their deathbed—they never say "I wish I'd been more productive." They say "I wish I'd spent more time with people I love." There's something to that, but you can't just not work; that's not realistic. So the question becomes how to find the balance. I don't think anyone's really figured that out yet. Maybe the answer is different for everyone. It's just something I've been thinking about lately, especially with everything going on.
