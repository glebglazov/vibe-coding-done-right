My grandmother used to make this amazing soup, and I've been trying to recreate it for years. The base is simple—just onions, carrots, celery, the usual. But the secret was miso paste, which is weird because she wasn't into Asian cooking at all. Someone must have told her about it at some point. She'd let it simmer for hours—not 30 minutes like the recipes online say, but three or four hours. The texture would get so thick, almost like a stew. I tried it last weekend and got close, but not quite right. I think the issue might be the potato type—she used waxy ones, not the russets I had.
