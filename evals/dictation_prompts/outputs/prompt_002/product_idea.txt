Okay, here's an idea I had. What if we built a browser extension that tracks how much time you spend on different sites? Instead of just showing a boring chart, it actually calculates how much money that time costs based on your hourly rate. So if you spend 45 minutes on Twitter, it would say, "Hey, that just cost you $37." I know there are time trackers out there already, but none of them do the money angle in a way that's like in your face about it. The psychological impact of seeing dollars instead of minutes could be really powerful, I think. Although some people might find it depressing, that's the point, right?
