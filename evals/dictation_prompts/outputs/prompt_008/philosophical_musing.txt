We spend so much time optimizing for productivity, but what are we even being productive for? I read an article recently that said people on their deathbed never say they wish they'd been more productive—they say they wish they'd spent more time with people they love. And there's something to that, but you can't just not work. That's not realistic. So the question becomes how you find the balance, and I don't think anyone's really figured that out yet. Maybe the answer is different for everyone. It's just something I've been thinking about lately, especially with everything going on.
