My grandmother used to make an amazing soup, and I've been trying to recreate it for years. The base is simple—just onions, carrots, and celery, the usual. The secret was miso paste, which is weird since she wasn't into Asian cooking. But someone must have told her about it. She'd let it simmer for hours—not the 30 minutes recipes online suggest, but three or four hours. The texture would get thick, almost like a stew. I tried it last weekend and it was close but not quite right. I think the issue is the potato type. She used waxy ones; I've been using russets.
