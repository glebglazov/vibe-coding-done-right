~ Transcription Override
  A Mode's departure from the Default Transcription Model. It names a model, and may additionally carry settings keyed by that model's Transcription Model Family — so a Mode may override the model alone and inherit its family's settings, or override individual settings too, with anything unset falling through to the family's own. It is macOS-only, because Modes are.
