+ Transcription Override
  A Mode's departure from the Default Transcription Model. It names a model, and may additionally carry that model's settings keyed by the same model — so a Mode may override the model alone and inherit its settings, or override individual settings too, with anything unset falling through to that model's own. It is macOS-only, because Modes are.
